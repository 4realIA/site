About

I've spent over 20 years on the defensive side of security, in defense and aerospace, fintech, banking and healthcare. Today I work as a virtual CISO and consultant, helping firms build security programs that hold up to auditors, clients and attackers alike.

I've owned SOC 1 and SOC 2 Type II audits end to end, briefed boards on risk, and run incident response tabletop exercises with executive teams. I spend most of my time translating technical findings into business decisions.

My roots in the defense industry still shape how I work. As an ISSO and ISSM, I worked within the NIST Risk Management Framework, from categorizing systems and selecting controls through assessment and continuous monitoring. I've also performed CMMC Level 2 gap assessments, measuring environments against the 110 requirements of NIST SP 800-171 and laying out a clear remediation plan.

More and more of my work is about AI: assessing AI vendors, writing acceptable-use policies, and mapping AI risk for firms that handle privileged information. I'm preparing for ISACA's AI security management credential (AAISM), and I run AI models locally in my own apps, so I know what "the data never leaves the device" takes in practice.

I build the tools I wish existed, usually for someone specific: a language app for my daughter, a way for my mom to save the 8-hour fireplace videos she loves, a PDF combiner for homework nights. Each one gets the treatment I'd expect from a vendor I was assessing: signed releases, a strict content security policy, no telemetry, and an independent code review before release.

If we've worked together, you've probably seen the Diet Mountain Dew. Yes, that's what the logo is.
